# Conference paper

**Paper:** Yiyang Wang, “Research on the Impact of Tariff Incidents on the Stock and Gold Markets: An Empirical Analysis Based on the ARIMA Model.”

**Venue:** 2025 5th International Conference on Economic Management and Corporate Governance (EMCG 2025).

**Status:** Accepted and included in the conference proceedings.

## Abstract

The study examines the closing prices of the Shanghai Stock Exchange Composite Index and the London Gold Fix around the April 2025 tariff event. ARIMA models are estimated using pre-event observations, and post-event actual prices are compared with forecasts representing the no-tariff counterfactual. The reported results show a negative short-term deviation for the stock index and a positive short-term deviation for gold.
